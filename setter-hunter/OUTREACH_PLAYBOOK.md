# Setter Hunter Outreach Playbook

## Qualification score
- Setter experience: 20
- High-ticket: 15
- DM/outbound: 15
- Proven results: 20
- Availability: 10
- Commercial profile: 10
- Relevant niche: 10

## Priority
- 80-100: priority
- 60-79: interesting
- 40-59: secondary
- <40: reject

## Sequence
1. Initial personalized message
2. Follow-up after 2 days if no reply
3. Follow-up after 5 days if no reply
4. Close-loop message after 9 days

## Guardrails
Use official APIs and authorized platform workflows where available. Do not scrape private data, bypass rate limits, evade platform controls, or automate prohibited account actions. Keep human approval for channels where direct automation is not authorized.
